## The problem

Data centers need huge amounts of power, and the regular grid connection process takes years. A new kind of deal has started showing up: connect much sooner, but accept that the grid operator can cut your power when the system is under stress. Nobody tells you how often that will actually happen, so companies are signing multi year, multi million dollar contracts with no real way to size the risk.

## What we built

Fluxline turns that unknown into a real number. Tell it your location, how much power you need, and how much of your load can pause when asked, and it shows you how often you'd realistically get cut off, what that costs you over the life of the contract, and whether connecting early is actually worth it compared to waiting for a normal connection.

It also ties this to sustainability. The hours a site gets cut off aren't random, they're exactly the hours the grid is under the most stress, which is when the dirtiest power plants are running. So a flexible connection doesn't just save time, it also shifts demand away from the dirtiest power on the system, and Fluxline measures that shift instead of just claiming it.

Every number Fluxline shows is either backed by real public grid data or clearly labeled as an assumption. We never fabricate a score.
